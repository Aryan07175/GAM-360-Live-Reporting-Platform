# Phase 6 — Yield & Programmatic Intelligence

## Overview
Phase 6 equips Ask GAM 360 with Yield Management & Programmatic Operations capabilities. It enables yield managers, programmatic analysts, and revenue teams to monitor, audit, and optimize monetization across Google Ad Exchange, Open Bidding yield partners, AdSense, Unified Pricing Rules, and Programmatic Deals in real time.

## Capabilities

### 1. Open Bidding & Mediation Yield Groups (`getYieldGroups`)
- **Query Engine**: Connects to GAM API `YieldGroupService`.
- **Supported Filters**:
  - `name_filter`: Filter yield groups by name (e.g., `App_OB`, `Web_OB`).
  - `type_filter`: Filter by yield integration type (`OPEN_BIDDING`, `MEDIATION`).
  - `format_filter`: Filter by inventory ad format (`BANNER`, `INTERSTITIAL`, `NATIVE`, `VIDEO`, `REWARDED`).
- **Returned Metadata**:
  - `yield_group_id` & `name`
  - `exchange_status` (`ACTIVE`, `INACTIVE`)
  - `format` & `environment_type`
  - `targeted_ad_units`: Configured ad unit inventory targeting.
  - `ad_sources`: List of yield partners (e.g., Google Open Bidding, Rubicon, PubMatic, InMobi) along with their integration type, company ID, and partner status.

### 2. Unified Pricing Rules & Video Ad Rules (`getPricingRules`)
- **Query Engine**: Connects to GAM API `AdRuleService`.
- **Supported Filters**:
  - `name_filter`: Filter ad rules by name.
  - `status_filter`: Filter by rule status (`ACTIVE`, `INACTIVE`).
- **Returned Metadata**:
  - `rule_id` & `name`
  - `priority` & `status`
  - `frequency_cap_behavior`
  - `targeted_ad_units` & `excluded_ad_units`
  - `start_date`

### 3. Programmatic Guaranteed, Preferred Deals & Auctions (`getProgrammaticDeals`)
- **Query Engine**: Connects to GAM API `ProposalLineItemService` and `ProposalService`.
- **Supported Filters**:
  - `name_filter`: Filter programmatic deals or proposals by name.
  - `deal_type`: Filter by programmatic deal type (`PREFERRED_DEAL`, `PRIVATE_AUCTION`, `PROGRAMMATIC_GUARANTEED`, `STANDARD`, `SPONSORSHIP`).
  - `status_filter`: Filter by reservation or proposal status (`APPROVED`, `DRAFT`, `FINALIZED`, `RESERVED`).
- **Returned Metadata**:
  - `deal_id` & `proposal_id`
  - `name` & `deal_type` (`lineItemType`)
  - `rate_type` (`CPM`, `CPC`, `CPD`) & `net_rate`
  - `contracted_units_bought`
  - `reservation_status` & `delivery_indicator`

### 4. Yield & Programmatic Monetization Analytics (`getYieldAnalytics`)
- **Reporting Engine**: Interfaces with Ask GAM 360's live reporting pipeline across three key programmatic dimensions:
  1. **`demand_channel`**: Compares performance across `Ad Exchange`, `Open Bidding`, `AdSense`, `Ad server` (Direct), and `SDK Bidding`.
  2. **`yield_group`**: Analyzes revenue and impressions generated by specific Open Bidding and mediation yield groups.
  3. **`programmatic_channel`**: Breaks down Ad Exchange programmatic delivery into `Open Auction`, `Private Auction`, `Preferred Deal`, and `Programmatic Guaranteed`.
- **Monetization Metrics**:
  - Total revenue ($) and impression volume per programmatic channel or yield partner.
  - Share of total monetization (%) per channel.
  - Effective CPM (`eCPM`) across demand channels.

## Target Users
- **Yield Managers**: Audit Open Bidding partner performance, identify underperforming yield groups, and monitor Unified Pricing Rule floor impacts.
- **Programmatic Teams**: Track delivery of Programmatic Guaranteed deals and audit active Preferred Deals and Private Auctions.
- **Revenue Leadership**: Understand the revenue split between Direct sales, Ad Exchange open auctions, and third-party Open Bidding yield partners.
